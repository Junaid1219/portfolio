01 / About

Engineering systems that scale under pressure.

I build production systems where the cost of failure is real — banking transactions that can't double-debit, clinical data that can't leak, retail platforms that can't buckle on Black Friday.

My approach pairs Spring Boot microservices on the backend with thoughtful React and Angular front-ends, glued together by Kafka and RabbitMQ event streams and deployed on AWS with proper observability. I lean on TDD because catching a double-debit in a Saga rollback during code review is cheaper than catching it in production.

Currently completing my M.S. in Computer Science at the University of Central Missouri while leading the legacy modernization effort at First Citizens Bank.
